[Company name] ("we") runs the Zentrix app and its online service. This policy explains what we collect, why, and
the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **Account data:** when you sign in with Google, your name, email address and Google account identifier. You can
  use most of the app without an account.
- **Your content:** trades you record in the journal (prices, sizes, notes), watchlists, alerts and settings
  (language, theme, risk profile).
- **Device data:** a push-notification token from Firebase Cloud Messaging, so we can send the alerts you set up.
  We also record the app version.
- **Technical data:**
  - Your IP address is processed when you use the service, to protect it from abuse (rate limiting). It is kept
    only briefly.
  - Security events (such as sign-ins and account deletion) are logged. After you delete your account, only a
    one-way hash of your account identifier stays in that log.
  - If error reporting is enabled in your app version, crash reports with technical details of the error are
    collected. They contain no name, email, location or account data.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, or any bank, card or trading-account details. We do not
connect to your broker or exchange.

## Why we use it

- To provide the service: your account, journal, alerts and settings.
- To keep it secure and working: abuse protection and fixing errors.
- To improve the analysis tools, using aggregated, non-identifying usage figures.

We do **not** sell your data, show ads, or use your data for advertising.

## Who processes it for us

These providers process data only on our instructions:

- **Firebase Cloud Messaging:** notifications.

Market data and news come from third parties (exchanges, data vendors, central banks, news publishers). We send
them no personal data.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's encrypted storage. Access to our
systems is restricted and logged.
